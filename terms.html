A personal, non-transferable licence to use Ringzo on Apple devices you own, under Apple's Standard EULA. No copying, reselling or reverse engineering.

Whose sounds can I cut?

Only audio you own or have the right to use. You are responsible for what you turn into a ringtone. Protected Apple Music files cannot be cut. Setting a ringtone needs iOS 26 or later.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: 3 ringtones up to 15 seconds each. Pro: 30-second tones, fades and unlimited ringtones. Weekly at US$4.99 with a 3-day free trial for eligible users, or yearly at US$34.99. Limited-time offers may appear in the app; other countries use Apple's local prices.

How does billing work?

Your Apple ID is charged on confirmation. Plans renew by themselves unless you turn auto-renew off at least 24 hours before the period ends, in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. Unused trial days are forfeited when you start a paid plan. Refunds are Apple's call.
