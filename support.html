Ringzo Support

How do I set my ringtone?

Save a ringtone in Ringzo, tap Set as Ringtone, then Use as Ringtone in the share sheet (iOS 26 or later). iPhone adds it to Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Ringtone.

For one person only

Contacts › the person › Edit › Ringtone, and pick your Ringzo sound.

Why can't I cut a song?

Apple Music and other protected files can't be cut. Use a video you filmed, a voice recording or your own audio file.

Restore a purchase

Settings in Ringzo › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.
