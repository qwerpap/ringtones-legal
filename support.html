Ringzo support

Stuck? Start here, then write to us.

"Use as Ringtone" is missing.

That button needs iOS 26 or newer. Update iOS, then reopen the ringtone.

My file will not open.

Files protected by Apple Music (DRM) cannot be cut. Use your own recording, a video from Photos or an unprotected audio file.

Ringtone not in Settings › Sounds.

Save first, then tap "Use as Ringtone". The name you gave it is what shows up.

Restore or cancel a plan?

Restore in the app's Settings. Cancel in iPhone Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
