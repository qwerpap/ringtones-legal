Ringzo Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Ringzo turns a sound from a video, a recording or an audio file into a ringtone. Everything happens on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Videos you pick from Photos, audio files you open from Files and your microphone recordings are processed only on your iPhone. Ringzo never uploads them. The ringtones you make are saved in the app's own storage on your device and in your iPhone's ringtone list when you choose “Use as Ringtone”.

Ringzo uses Photos through the system picker (only the video you choose is shared with the app) and the microphone only while you record.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To check your subscription and measure how many people start a trial, Ringzo uses Adapty (adapty.io), which receives an anonymous app user ID, your device model, OS version, the purchases you make and anonymous onboarding steps (for example, which answer you picked in the welcome questions). Adapty does not receive your sounds, videos or recordings. Adapty's privacy policy: https://adapty.io/privacy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

There is no sign-in, no advertising SDK and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Ringzo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Delete a ringtone in the app with the × button, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. To ask Adapty to delete your purchase data, email us.
