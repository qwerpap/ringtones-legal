Ringzo privacy, as questions and answers

Short, plain, and specific to a ringtone maker.

Do my videos or recordings get uploaded?

No. Videos you choose from Photos, audio files you open from Files and microphone recordings are processed on your iPhone only. Ringzo has no server for your sounds.

What access does Ringzo ask for?

Photos through the system picker: the app receives only the video you pick, not your library. The microphone only while you record, and only after you allow it. Files access only for the file you choose.

Where do my ringtones end up?

In Ringzo's own storage on the phone and, when you tap "Use as Ringtone", in iOS's list of tones. Delete one with the × button, or delete the app to clear everything.

Who sees my purchases?

Apple processes payments. Adapty (adapty.io) gets a random user ID, device model, OS version, purchase status and anonymous welcome-question answers so subscriptions and trials work. It never receives sounds, videos or recordings. Adapty's policy.

Any account, ads or tracking?

No sign-in, no ad SDK, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Children?

Not aimed at under-13s; we do not knowingly collect their data.
